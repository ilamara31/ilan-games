Privacy Policy

Ilan Games — covers the Stack Tower iPhone and iPad app and the games on this website. Last updated 13 September 2026.

The short version: you can play without an account and nothing at all is sent anywhere. If you choose to create an account to appear on the leaderboard, we store a username, a hashed password, and your scores. Nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What is collected

DataWhenWhy
UsernameOnly if you create an accountTo show who holds a score on the leaderboard
PasswordOnly if you create an accountTo prove it is you. Stored only as a bcrypt hash — the plain password is never saved and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us
Game scoresOnly while signed inTo place you on the leaderboard

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifiers or IDFA
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No cookies for tracking, and no third-party trackers

Tracking and advertising

There is none. Your data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers, data brokers or any third party. Nothing is used to build a profile of you or to follow you across other apps and websites.

Playing without an account

Signing in is entirely optional. If you do not sign in, the app makes no network requests at all — your best score is kept only on your own device, and nothing leaves it.

Where it is stored

Account details and scores are stored on Supabase, a hosted database provider acting on our behalf. Data is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash.

The leaderboard is public within the games: anyone playing can see the usernames and scores on it. Please do not use a username you would not want shown publicly, and do not reuse a password from anywhere else.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app: open Sign in from the title screen, then tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your scores from the leaderboard. It cannot be undone, and no copy is retained.

Children

These games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information beyond a username you choose yourself. We do not knowingly collect information from children beyond that. If you believe a child has created an account you would like removed, use the in-app deletion above or get in touch.

Your rights

You can see everything held about you — it is your username and your scores, both visible on the leaderboard — and you can erase all of it yourself using Delete account. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data, or a request to remove an account you cannot sign in to: open an issue on GitHub.
