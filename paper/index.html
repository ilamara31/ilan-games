Paper Territory

Leave your zone, draw a loop, come home — and the land inside is yours.
How it works
Swipe, use the arrow keys or the on-screen pad to steer. Leave your zone to draw a trail, then return to claim everything inside. If a rival crosses your trail you pop — and never cross your own trail or hit the glowing wall.
