How to play

The goal

Trap the other king so it can't escape. That's checkmate and you win. If a king is attacked it's in check, and its owner must get out of check right away.

How the pieces move

Special moves

  • Castling: move your king two squares toward a rook, and the rook jumps over. Only if neither has moved, nothing is between them, and the king isn't in, through, or into check.
  • En passant: if a pawn jumps two squares and lands right beside your pawn, you can take it as if it moved one square, but only on the very next move.
  • Promotion: a pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

Draws

  • Stalemate: the player to move has no legal move but isn't in check.
  • The same position 3 times, 50 moves each with no capture or pawn move, or not enough pieces left to checkmate.
  • Both players agree to a draw.

Clocks

Each player has their own clock. It only runs on your turn, starting after your first move. Run out of time and you lose, unless your opponent can't possibly checkmate you (then it's a draw). In "15 | 10" you get 10 extra seconds after every move.

Ratings

Everyone starts at 500. Only online games against real people change your rating. Beat a stronger player to gain more points. Games against bots and on the same device count in your stats but not your rating.

4 Player chess

Red, Blue, Yellow and Green play on a big cross-shaped board, taking turns clockwise. Everyone is against everyone! Win by scoring the most points.

  • Checkmate a player and they're out. Their pieces turn grey: they never move, block the way, and are worth 0 points.
  • Check two kings with one move: +5. Three kings: +20.
  • Pawns become a queen in the middle of the board (worth only 1 point). No en passant.
  • Stalemated? You're out, but you get +20. Run out of time or resign and you're out with no bonus.
  • The game ends when one player is left. Most points wins, even if you were knocked out!

Moving pieces

Tap a piece, then tap where it should go. Or drag it. Dots show the squares it can move to.
