HOW TO PLAY

Everyone joins the same room on their own phone and talks on a call (like Google Meet). The game is the God: it hands out secret roles, runs the night, and tells everyone what happened.

The roles

🔪

Mafia — each night the Mafia pick someone to kill. They know who the other Mafia are. By day, they pretend to be normal.

💉

Doctor (one) — each night picks one person to save. If the Mafia picked that person, they live.

🔍

Detective (one) — each night picks one person and secretly learns: Mafia or not.

🌾

Villager — no night power. Talk, guess, and vote out the Mafia.

How many Mafia?

4–6 players: 1 · 7–8: 2 · 9–11: 3 · 12–14: 4 · 15: 5. Always 1 Doctor and 1 Detective. Everyone else is a Villager.

A round

  • Night — everyone "closes their eyes". Then it's the Mafia's turn (they must all pick the same person), then the Doctor's turn, then the Detective's turn.
  • Morning — God tells who the Mafia killed (or that the Doctor saved them), and whether the Detective found a Mafia.
  • Discussion — talk on your call and in the chat. Who's lying?
  • Vote — tap who you want out. Most votes is out (a tie means nobody). God reveals what they really were.

Who wins?

The Village wins when every Mafia is out. The Mafia wins when there are as many Mafia as everyone else.

If you're out, stay quiet on the call — no hints!
