Three ways the home page could look

Same 27 games, same art, three different rooms to put them in. Open each on a laptop if you can — they all respond to where the cursor is.

1 · Neon Vault

A console dashboard. Big cinematic banner that cycles through featured games, then shelves you scroll sideways. Cards lean toward your cursor and the art inside them shifts behind the frame.

Open →

2 · Arcade Floor

The games stand as objects on a lit floor that runs back to a horizon. The whole room leans as you move, each one casts a shadow, and holding on one lifts it off the ground.

Open →

3 · The Index

Almost no boxes at all. A printed-looking list of every game; run your eye down it and the artwork behind the whole page changes to match the line you're on.

Open →

Nothing here touches the real home page — it all lives in /preview/ and nobody can reach it from the arcade. The artwork is drawn in code rather than painted, so it stays sharp at any size and weighs almost nothing.
